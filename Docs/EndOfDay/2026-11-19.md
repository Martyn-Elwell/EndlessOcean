## Work in detail

### Swimming, breath and health (`Source/EndlessOcean/Player/`, `Health/`)
- **Swimming:** surface swimming, diving and rising (`IA_SwimUp`). Stamina doubles as an underwater sprint.
- **`UBreathComponent`:** breath drains underwater. Once it's empty, drowning damage kills in
  **`DrowningTimeToDeath` = 5 s**. Damage per tick scales with the owner's `UHealthComponent` max health, so the
  5 s stays true if max health is retuned.
- **`UDamageScreenEffectComponent`** drives `M_PP_DamageVignette`:
  - a black ring that closes while drowning;
  - a red pulse on each hit.
  - At full health both are exactly 0. The low-health heartbeat and lasting blood are still available but off by
    default.
- **Material bug fixed:** the vignette used aspect-corrected distance, so wide screens showed black corners even
  at 0.
- **Underwater look:** `BP_UnderwaterCameraModifier`.

### Weapons and items (`Source/EndlessOcean/Items/`)
- **SPP-1 underwater pistol (`BP_SPP1`):**
  - four barrels, separate rounds (`BP_AmmoSPP1`) kept in an ammo pouch;
  - aim down sights on RMB (`IA_AltUse`), reload (`IA_Reload`), sway, spread and recoil.
  - Range falls with depth (20 m at the surface to 5 m at 40 m).
- **Wrench (`BP_Wrench`):** slow, heavy melee that costs stamina. Can't be used underwater.
- **Target dummy** for testing damage.
- **Real-size models (playtest fix):**
  - the gun is about 23 x 13 cm and the round about 14.5 cm, built from primitives;
  - the cause of "too big" was placed copies spawned at scale 1, which overrode the Blueprint root scale.
- **Held items no longer block the player (playtest fix):**
  - every part except the root mesh is visual-only (`NoCollision`);
  - `AHeldItem` also turns off collision on all parts while an item is carried, and restores it when dropped.

### HUD (`UI_SurvivorHUD`)
- The reticle was a 24 pt "." text scaled by the gun's spread, so it was big, pulsed and sat low. It's now a
  4 px round image that only fades out while aiming.

### Camera and interaction (late session)
- **Pitch limit:** `AEndlessOceanCameraManager` now goes from -70/+80 to **±88°**, effectively straight down
  without the camera flipping.
- **`UInteractionComponent` aim assist:**
  - if the view sweep finds nothing usable, it picks the interactable whose bounds the view ray passes closest
    to, within 10° or 20 cm;
  - the bounds include visual-only parts like the pistol's grip;
  - there's a line-of-sight check, and "stickiness" so the prompt doesn't flicker;
  - reach is 250 cm and the sweep radius 6 cm;
  - debug: `eo.Interaction.Debug 1` (a yellow box marks a pick made by aim assist).

### Water (late session)
- **`/Game/Environment/Water/M_Water_Temp`:** translucent and two-sided, with an animated caustic pattern and
  wave normals in Custom HLSL nodes, and tunable parameters.
- **`Water_Surface`:** an engine plane over the pool and shaft in `Lvl_SwimTest`.
- **First version acted as a floor:** `bUseDefaultCollision` was still on, so the mesh's BlockAll collision
  overrode NoCollision. Fixed (`72d1121`).
- **Saving gotcha:** property edits made through the MCP tools don't mark an actor as unsaved, so Ctrl+S skipped
  them. The fix only saved after the actor was placed again.

### Decisions made today
- **The revolver became the SPP-1:** a four-barrel underwater pistol, with separate pickable rounds.
- **Plan v1 rejected, v2 approved:**
  - the ammo pouch cap went from 12 to 4 spare rounds;
  - the health bar was dropped in favour of on-screen damage effects;
  - fall damage and a debug damage console variable were added.
- **Vignette only while taking damage;** low breath alone no longer darkens the screen, since the HUD breath bar
  covers it.
- **Drowning is time-based** (5 s to death) rather than a fixed damage per second.
- **Pickup leniency through bounds-based aim assist,** not a bigger sweep (it would hit the floor first when
  looking down) or per-item grab boxes (extra setup on every item).
- **Held-item root as the only collider.** Considered and deferred: a `UBoxComponent` root for all items. It's
  cleaner, but it touches every item; worth revisiting when real meshes arrive.
- **The water is temporary:** to be replaced by a proper water material or the Water plugin in the visual pass.
