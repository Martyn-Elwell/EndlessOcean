# Swimming, breath, SPP-1 underwater pistol and wrench (v5)
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Status: Done (merged into `main`; items listed under Verification as not yet tested still need a playtest)
- Changes from v4: the held-gun movement and reticle fixes made after v4, plus three new playtest notes:
  1. "Why can the camera not look completely down, it should be able to."
  2. "The picking up of items needs to be more lenient. How you do this is up to you."
  3. "The water should have a texture even if it's temporary."

Everything in v4 still stands except where this version says otherwise.

## Fixes made after v4 (already committed)

- **"When you hold the gun the movement breaks."** The gun's extra parts still had collision, so the held gun
  blocked the player's own capsule. Every part except the root is now `NoCollision` in the Blueprints, and
  `AHeldItem` also switches off collision on all parts while an item is carried and restores it when dropped.
- **"The reticle, centre dot, is large and changes size."** The dot was a 24 pt "." text glyph scaled by the
  pistol's spread. It's now a fixed 4 px round image that only fades out while aiming.

## 1. Looking straight down

`AEndlessOceanCameraManager` (template code) clamped pitch to -70 / +80 degrees. Now **-88 / +88**.

**Why not 90:** at exactly +-90 the view's "up" direction becomes undefined and the camera can flip, and the camera
modifiers add a small bob on top of the clamped aim.

## 2. More lenient pickups

`UInteractionComponent` now searches in two passes:
1. The existing sphere sweep down the view (radius 4 → **6 cm**, reach 200 → **250 cm**). The first thing it hits
   wins, so walls still hide what's behind them.
2. **Aim assist**, only if pass 1 found nothing usable. It finds every interactable within reach with one overlap
   query. For each, it works out how far the view ray misses the item's bounds, including visual-only parts with no
   collision (the pistol's grip). It keeps those within **`AssistAngle` (10°) or `AssistRadius` (20 cm)**, best
   first, and takes the first one with a clear line of sight.
   - **`FocusStickiness` (1.5x):** the item already in focus keeps it until it falls outside 1.5x those limits.
     This stops the prompt flickering.
   - **`bAimAssist`** turns pass 2 off.
   - **Debug:** `eo.Interaction.Debug 1` draws a yellow box around an item picked by aim assist.

**Why this way:** the thin root collision (on the pistol, the 2.4 cm barrel housing) was the real reason pickups felt
fiddly. Bounds include every visible part, so "looking at any bit of it" counts.

**Considered:**
- A much bigger trace sphere. Rejected: it hits the floor and walls first, so it gets worse when looking down at
  items.
- A per-item invisible "grab" collision box. Rejected: it needs setting up on every item and has the same wall
  problem.

## 3. Temporary water texture

The water in `Lvl_SwimTest` was only invisible physics volumes. Added:
- **`/Game/Environment/Water/M_Water_Temp`:** translucent, two-sided, lit per pixel.
  - Two Custom HLSL nodes: an animated caustic ridge pattern (base colour, plus a faint emissive glow so it reads in
    a dark ship) and the normal of four travelling sine waves.
  - Parameters in the `Water` group: `DeepColor`, `ShallowColor`, `CausticScale`, `CausticSpeed`, `GlowStrength`,
    `WaveStrength`, `Opacity`, `Roughness`.
- **`Water_Surface`** (folder `SwimTest/Water`): an engine plane over the pool and shaft at z = -40. It has no
  collision and casts no shadow.

**Replace later** with a proper water material and meshes (or the Water plugin) in the visual pass.
