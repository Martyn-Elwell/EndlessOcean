# Swimming, breath, SPP-1 underwater pistol and wrench (v4)
- Date: 2026-11-19
- Status: Approved (built on `player-character`; first playtest fixes done, awaiting the next playtest)
- Changes from v3: fixes from the user's first hands-on playtest of `Lvl_SwimTest`:
  1. "The primitive objects for the bullets and guns are not good, they are too big and do not represent the look
     like the objects they are trying to represent."
  2. "The vignette should only appear when the player is taking damage. There should be no vignette when the player
     is at full health."
  3. "When you are out of breath you should lose health faster, 5s from being out of breath to death."

Everything in v3 still stands except where this version says otherwise.

## 1. Item size and look

**Cause of "too big":** the items placed in `Lvl_SwimTest` (gun, 8 rounds, wrench, 2 flares) were spawned through MCP
with an actor scale of 1. A placed actor's scale *is* its root component's scale, so it replaced the small root scale
set in each Blueprint: the gun was about a metre across. Dragging a Blueprint into the level from the Content Browser
doesn't have this problem (the editor uses the Blueprint's root scale), and neither does `SpawnActor` at runtime.

**Fix:** every placed copy now uses its Blueprint's root scale, and the models were rebuilt at real size:

| Item | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `BP_SPP1` | A box, a block and a grip | About 23 x 13 cm, like the real gun: barrel housing (the root and physics body, 14 x 2.4 x 2.4 cm) with four 1.2 cm barrels poking out, frame, hammer, raked grip, trigger guard, trigger, front and rear sights |
| `BP_AmmoSPP1` | A 2.5 x 8 cm cylinder | About 14.5 cm, like the real round: a 1.1 x 4 cm case (root and physics body), rim, thin 10 cm steel dart with a point |

- The new parts use **absolute scale** (`bAbsoluteScale`), so the root's non-uniform scale doesn't stretch or shear
  them (the grip is tilted). Their locations are still in the root's scaled space.
- `AimLocationOffset` z raised from 9 to 11 cm, because the sights now sit about 2 cm above the bore instead of 4.6 cm.
- The muzzle sits at the front of the barrel cluster (8.2 cm forward of the root).

**Editor lesson recorded for later MCP work:** adding parts to a Blueprint and then setting their properties left
the copies already placed in the level with empty parts. Recompiling treated their old values as per-instance
overrides. Placed copies had to be deleted and placed again.

**Considered and not done:** a `UBoxComponent` root for all held items (so a mesh never carries the item's size in
its scale). It's cleaner, but it changes `AHeldItem` for every item. It's worth doing if the visual pass brings real
meshes with their own pivots.

## 2. Vignette only while taking damage

**Bug:** `M_PP_DamageVignette` measured distance with aspect correction, so on a wide screen the corners were further
out than the black ring's starting edge. It drew black corners even at `DrownAmount = 0`. The distance is now
normalised to the screen shape (0 at the centre, 1 in the corners), both effects start beyond the corners, and the
drowning ring is also multiplied by its own amount, so 0 is exactly nothing.

**Behaviour change in `UDamageScreenEffectComponent`:**
- **Black drowning ring:** only while drowning damage is being taken. It starts at `DrownAmountAtStart` (0.3) and
  closes completely as that damage takes the health away. Low breath alone no longer closes it (the HUD breath bar
  covers that). `DrownStartBreath` and `DrownAmountAtNoBreath` were removed.
- **Red blood:** a pulse on each hit that fades away.
- **Off by default, still available:** the lasting blood level at low health (`PersistentBloodAtZeroHealth` = 0) and
  the low-health heartbeat (new `bHeartbeat` = false, with its settings hidden unless it's ticked).

## 3. Drowning kills in 5 seconds

`UBreathComponent`:
- `DrowningDamagePerSecond` is replaced by **`DrowningTimeToDeath`** (5 s).
- Each tick deals `MaxHealth x DrowningDamageInterval / DrowningTimeToDeath`, using the owner's `UHealthComponent`
  (`FallbackMaxHealth` 100 otherwise). The design value stays true if MaxHealth is retuned; already-injured players
  die sooner.
- `DrowningDamageInterval` drops from 1.0 to 0.5 s, so the ring closes in ten smooth steps.
