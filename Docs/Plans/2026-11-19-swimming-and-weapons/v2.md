# Swimming, breath, SPP-1 underwater pistol and wrench (v2)
- Date: 2026-11-19
- Status: Superseded by v3
- Changes from v1: ammo pouch cap 12 -> 4 spare bullets; health bar removed and replaced by a damage screen-effect component (black closing vignette for drowning, red blood vignette for physical damage, heartbeat pulse at low health); added damage types (drowning, fall), fall damage on landing, optional slow health regeneration, and a debug damage cvar.

## Context
The survivor (merged 19 Nov: walk/sprint/crouch, stamina, one hand plus 3-slot inventory, flare) needs to handle a
flooding ship and threats. This adds:
- **Swimming:** surface swimming, diving and rising, an underwater sprint that uses stamina, and a breath meter.
- **The SPP-1 underwater pistol:** 4 barrels, loaded one round at a time, with heavy sway and spread, aim-down-sights,
  big recoil, and a slow rate of fire. Its range shrinks with depth: 20 m at the surface down to 5 m at 40 m.
- **A large wrench:** slow sway, slow swings with a long delay, and each swing costs half the stamina bar.
Everything uses grey primitives. Hand poses and animation come in the later visual pass.

Decisions from the user:
- **RMB = the held item's alternate action** (the pistol aims, the flare throws). Throwing also gets its own key, **G**.
- **Drowning:** out of breath drains a new reusable **Health** component. At 0 the player dies and the level restarts.
- **No health bar.** Damage shows as screen effects: **drowning = a black vignette closing in**; **everything else**
  (falling, cuts, monster attacks) = **a red, blood-like vignette**.
- **Scarce resources:** the ammo pouch holds only **4 spare bullets**.
- **Underwater:** the flare and the SPP-1 work underwater. The wrench and most other items are lowered and can't be used.
- **Bullets:** go into a separate **ammo pouch** (a counted resource shown on the HUD), not into inventory slots.

**Scope:** large, about 5 days. The deadline isn't at risk on its own, but the monster AI hasn't started and is the
bigger risk. Build in the order below so each step is playable on its own. Work continues on `player-character`, as
the user requested. Once approved, this plan goes into `Docs/Plans/2026-11-19-swimming-and-weapons/` per the
paper-trail rule.

## Shared foundations (do first)

### `Source/EndlessOcean/Water/` (new folder)
- **`WaterQueryLibrary.h/.cpp`: `UWaterQueryLibrary : UBlueprintFunctionLibrary`.** `GetWaterDepthAt(WorldContext,
  Location, OutVolume)` returns the depth below the surface (0 when out of water). It walks
  `UWorld::GetNonDefaultPhysicsVolumeIterator()`, takes volumes with `bWaterVolume` that `EncompassesPoint`, and uses
  the volume's top Z as the surface height. Water uses plain engine `APhysicsVolume`s (Water Volume ticked), so
  flooding can later raise or move them without code changes. Shared by breath, the pistol's range, and the camera.

### `Source/EndlessOcean/Health/` (new)
- **`HealthComponent.h/.cpp`: `UHealthComponent`.** `MaxHealth` and current health. Binds to the owner's engine
  `OnTakeAnyDamage`, so anything can hurt it with the standard `UGameplayStatics::ApplyDamage`/`ApplyPointDamage` (Unity
  comparison: an `IDamageable` that the engine routes to). Delegates `OnHealthChanged` and `OnDeath`, plus `bIsDead`.
  The monster and a test dummy reuse it.
- **Damage types:** `UDrowningDamageType` and `UFallDamageType` (both `UDamageType`). Anything that isn't drowning
  (fall, hazard, monster) counts as "physical" for the screen effects.
- **Optional slow regeneration** (`bRegenerate`, `RegenDelay` 8 s, `RegenRate`), on by default so the effects fade
  after a scare. It's one tick-box to turn off if healing should only come from items.
- **Fall damage:** `ASurvivorCharacter::Landed` applies `UFallDamageType` damage above `FallDamageMinSpeed` (curve
  from speed to damage). It covers falling through broken decks.
- **`Health/DamageScreenEffectComponent.h/.cpp` (on the player) plus `M_PP_DamageVignette`.**
  - Listens to `UHealthComponent` and `UBreathComponent` and drives one post-process material instance (a dynamic
    material in the camera's post-process blendables). No health bar anywhere.
  - **Drowning:** a **black vignette that closes in** as breath runs out, then tightens further with drowning damage.
    It opens up again at the surface.
  - **Physical damage:** a **red, blood-like vignette** (edge mask plus a noise/splatter texture, slightly darkened).
    A strong pulse on each hit fades back to a persistent level that scales with missing health. At low health a slow
    heartbeat pulse is added.
  - Parameters are exposed: colours, edge softness, pulse strength and fade times.
  - The material is built through MCP from engine textures, so it's placeholder art that can be replaced later.
- **Player death:** `ASurvivorPlayerController` binds `OnDeath`, disables input, fades out, and after
  `RestartDelay` calls `RestartLevel()`. This is a placeholder until checkpoints exist.

### `Source/EndlessOcean/Items/`: extend the hand (files created in this project)
- **`UItemDefinition`** gains:
  - `bUsableWhileSwimming`
  - Sway: `SwayRotationAmplitude` (deg), `SwayLocationAmplitude` (cm), `SwaySpeed`, `MovementSwayMultiplier`
  - `LoweredWhileSwimming` pose
- **`AHeldItem`** gains: `AltActionStarted/Completed` (BlueprintNativeEvent; default = nothing), `CancelActions()`
  (called when switching items, stowing or swimming), and `GetAdditivePose()`, so an item can add its own offset
  (aim pose, swing arc).
- **`UItemHolderComponent`:**
  - Routes alt start and stop. Blocks use and alt while swimming when `!bUsableWhileSwimming`.
  - Applies per-frame **procedural sway**: Perlin noise, scaled by movement speed, by the item's aim state, and lowered
    while swimming. Final pose = base offset + sway + the item's additive pose.
  - Tick runs **only while holding an item** (switched on and off).
  - Exposes `GetSwayRotation()` so the pistol can fire where the gun actually points.
- **Throw** moves to its own action (G). **`AFlareItem`** alt action = throw.
- **`UStaminaComponent`** gains `CanAfford(Amount)` and `TryConsume(Amount)`. The wrench uses these.

### Gameplay tags
Add `"GameplayTags"` to `EndlessOcean.Build.cs`. Native tags in **`Items/ItemTags.h/.cpp`**
(`UE_DEFINE_GAMEPLAY_TAG`): `Ammo.SPP1`. Native tags avoid hand-editing config files and are checked at compile time.

## 1. Swimming and breath (≈1.5 days)
- **`USurvivorMovementComponent`:**
  - Swimming defaults: `MaxSwimSpeed` ≈ 180, `Buoyancy` 1.0 (neutral), high fluid friction for weight.
  - New `SwimSprintSpeed`. `CanSprintNow()` also allows swimming (stamina drains as on land).
  - **Vertical control:** `SetSwimVerticalInput(float)` from the rise/dive keys. Above a `SurfaceHoldDepth` with no
    input, the character settles at the surface, head above water, so the player can rest and breathe.
  - **Climb out:** at the surface, holding rise against a ledge whose top is within `MaxClimbOutHeight` gives a short
    launch onto it. It uses the CMC water-jump check if it fits; otherwise a small sweep plus `LaunchCharacter`. This
    is checked during implementation.
- **`ASurvivorCharacter`:**
  - While swimming, W/S follow the camera's full direction (look down + W dives), and A/D strafe.
  - New inputs: **Rise (Space)** and **Dive (C/Ctrl)**. Crouch is ignored while swimming. Jump stays disabled.
  - On entering water, `ItemHolder` lowers items that can't be used while swimming and cancels any reload or aim.
- **`Player/BreathComponent.h/.cpp`: `UBreathComponent`.**
  - A timer (≈10 Hz) checks whether the eyes are underwater (`UWaterQueryLibrary` at the pawn's view location).
  - `MaxBreath` 40 s; drains under water and refills quickly at the surface (`RefillRate`).
  - At 0, deals `DrowningDamagePerSecond` to its owner through `ApplyDamage` with `UDrowningDamageType`.
  - Delegates `OnBreathChanged` and `OnSubmergedChanged`. No Tick.
- **`Player/UnderwaterCameraModifier.h/.cpp`.** While the camera is in water, blends a post-process (blue-green tint,
  fog-like falloff, slight blur and vignette) with a configurable fade. A camera modifier keeps the effect next to the
  existing camera feel and avoids placing post-process volumes in every flooded room.
- **HUD:** a breath bar (visible only while submerged or not full). There's no health bar; health shows through the
  vignettes.

## 2. Wrench, melee (≈1 day)
**`Items/Weapons/MeleeWeaponItem.h/.cpp`: `AMeleeWeaponItem : AHeldItem`.** Generic, so a pipe or crowbar later is
just a Blueprint.
- Use (LMB): if `Stamina->TryConsume(StaminaCostFraction * Max)` (0.5) and not cooling down, run the swing:
  **wind-up** (0.45 s) → **strike** → **recovery** (1.2 s), all on timers.
  - The procedural swing arc is the additive pose (back, then a fast arc across, then a slow return), shaped by a
    `UCurveFloat` so it can be tuned without code.
  - With too little stamina: a short "strain" pose instead, and no swing.
- **Strike:** a sphere sweep (`Reach` 160 cm, `Radius` 20 cm) from the camera, on a new **`Melee` trace channel**.
  `ApplyPointDamage`, an impulse on physics bodies, an impact sound, and an AI noise report.
- Slow, heavy idle sway comes from the definition.
- The definition is `TwoHanded` and `bUsableWhileSwimming = false`.
- **`BP_Wrench`:** grey box handle plus a cylinder head. **`DA_Wrench`.**
- Later (not this plan): the wrench as the tool repair points require. It's a natural link to the repair loop.

## 3. SPP-1 underwater pistol (≈2 days)
**`Items/Weapons/FirearmItem.h/.cpp`: `AFirearmItem : AHeldItem`.** All numbers are configurable, so the SPP-1 is a
Blueprint/data set-up and other guns stay possible.
- **Chambers:** `NumBarrels` 4, `LoadedRounds`, and `AmmoType` (gameplay tag `Ammo.SPP1`).
- **Reload (R):** opens the gun (`OpenTime` 0.8 s), then loads **one round every `PerRoundLoadTime` (1.2 s)** from the
  pouch until full or out, then closes (0.6 s).
  - Firing, sprinting, switching items or pressing R again interrupts it. Interrupting skips the close pose but keeps
    the rounds already loaded.
  - The HUD shows reload progress.
- **Fire (LMB):** `FireInterval` 1.4 s (untrained user). An empty gun gives a dry click.
  - **Direction** = camera rotation + the hand's current sway rotation + a random cone.
  - **Spread:** `HipSpread` 6° and `AimedSpread` 1.2°, scaled up by movement and recent recoil.
  - **Hitscan** from the camera, out to `GetEffectiveRange()`.
  - **Range:** `Lerp(RangeAtSurface 2000, RangeAtMaxDepth 500, Clamp(Depth / MaxRangeDepth 4000))`, using
    `UWaterQueryLibrary` at the muzzle. Out of water it's the surface range. The curve is exposed (`UCurveFloat`
    optional) so the falloff can be shaped.
  - Hits: `ApplyPointDamage`, an impulse, and a debug tracer (cvar `eo.Weapons.Debug`).
  - Feedback: a muzzle flash (brief point light) and a gunshot sound.
  - `UAISense_Hearing::ReportNoiseEvent` (loud) prepares for the monster's hearing.
- **Aim (RMB, hold):** blends to an aim-down-sights pose (gun centred) over `AimBlendTime` 0.25 s, narrows the FOV via
  the player controller (`AimFOV` 65), and multiplies sway by `AimedSwayMultiplier` 0.25 (never zero).
- **Recoil:** an instant control-rotation kick (`RecoilPitch` 7–10°, `RecoilYaw` ±2.5°). About 40% recovers over 0.35 s
  (Tick only while recovering). Also plays an optional `UCameraShakeBase` class set in the Blueprint, and a gun kick in
  the additive pose. The player is left aiming high, as intended.
- **Pickups:**
  - **`Items/AmmoPickup.h/.cpp`: `AAmmoPickup : AActor, IInteractable`.** `AmmoType`, `Count` (default 1 = a single
    bullet), "Pick up SPP-1 round".
  - **`Items/AmmoPouchComponent.h/.cpp`: `UAmmoPouchComponent`.** `TMap<FGameplayTag,int32>` counts with per-type
    caps (**`Ammo.SPP1` cap 4 spare**, so at most 8 rounds counting a loaded gun), `TryTake`/`Add`, `OnAmmoChanged`.
    Only the bullets that fit are taken from a pickup that holds more than one. If the pouch is full, the pickup stays put with the
    prompt "Ammo pouch full".
- **`BP_SPP1`:** grey box frame plus 4 small barrel cylinders. **`DA_SPP1`** (`bUsableWhileSwimming = true`).
  **`BP_AmmoSPP1`:** a tiny cylinder.
- **HUD:** a "2 / 4 · 7" ammo readout, the reload bar, and a crosshair that scales with spread and hides while aiming.

## Verification
- A clean editor build after each step.
- **Swimming:**
  - Entering the pool switches to swimming; at rest the head stays above water.
  - Space rises, C dives, and look-direction swimming works. Shift sprints underwater and drains stamina; exhaustion
    blocks it.
  - Climbing out at the ledge works.
- **Breath and health:**
  - Breath drains only with the eyes underwater, refills at the surface, and the HUD bar shows it.
  - At 0, health drains; at 0 health the screen fades and the level restarts.
  - There's no health bar. Running out of air closes a black vignette in. Falling from a height, or hitting yourself
    with the debug cvar `eo.Damage <amount>`, flashes a red blood vignette, which stays faintly at low health and fades
    with regeneration.
- **Underwater items:** the wrench is lowered and can't be used while swimming. The flare stays lit underwater and the
  SPP-1 still fires.
- **Wrench:**
  - Slow sway; LMB swings slowly and costs half the stamina bar.
  - It won't swing below half stamina.
  - It hits the dummy and knocks crates.
- **SPP-1:**
  - Picking up the gun and single rounds works; the pouch caps at 4 spare ("Ammo pouch full" after that); R loads one
    round at a time and can be interrupted.
  - Hip-fire sways and spreads widely. RMB aim narrows the FOV and cuts sway and spread without removing them.
  - Each shot kicks the camera up hard, about 1.4 s between shots, and a dry click when empty.
  - **Range:** a tracer stops at about 20 m at the surface, about 12.5 m at 20 m depth and about 5 m at 40 m+ (debug
    cvar), checked in the shaft.
  - The flare's RMB throws it, and G throws anything.
- **Details panel:** the new categories appear right under Transform.
