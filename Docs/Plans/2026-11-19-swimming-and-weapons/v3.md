# Swimming, breath, SPP-1 underwater pistol and wrench (v3)
- Date: 2026-11-19
- Status: Superseded by v4
- Changes from v2: records where the build differed from the approved plan, and what has and hasn't been tested.
  Everything else in v2 was built as described.

## Differences from v2

| v2 said | Built instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A `Melee` trace channel | One **`Weapon`** channel (GameTraceChannel3), used by the wrench and the pistol | Both need the same "what can a weapon hit" rules; one channel keeps the collision settings simpler. Triggers ignore it. |
| A separate `IA_SwimDown` input | **The crouch key (C/Ctrl) dives** while swimming | Crouching does nothing in water anyway, and it avoids two actions on the same key. |
| Sprinting interrupts a reload | **Not yet.** Firing, aiming, pressing R again or putting the gun away interrupt it | The item would need to know the owner's sprint state; left out to keep items independent of the player class. Easy to add if wanted. |
| "Neutral" buoyancy with a separate surface-hold | Two buoyancy values chosen each frame: **1.3 within 1.2 m of the surface** (you float up and rest with the eyes ~20 cm above water), **1.0 deeper** (hover) | Uses the engine's own swim physics instead of fighting it. |
| Flooded area added to `Lvl_ShipTest` | A separate **`Lvl_SwimTest`** (a copy of the ship test) with an annex behind the room: spawn platform, a 3 m pool with a 40 cm ledge, a 45 m shaft, the SPP-1, 8 rounds, the wrench, 2 flares, 3 physics crates, and 4 target dummies (platform, pool, 20 m and 40 m down the shaft) | Keeps the ship test map unchanged; the annex needed its own player start. |
| Translucent blue box as a water stand-in | **No water visual yet.** The underwater post-process shows when you're in water | The engine has no simple translucent material to hand, and an opaque surface plane turned out to be solid (the swimmer bumped into it). A proper water material is a visual-pass job. |
| `eo.Damage <amount>` | `eo.Damage <amount> [drown]` | The optional `drown` argument tests the black vignette without swimming. |

## Additions
- `UHealthComponent::Revive()` for the target dummies.
- `ATargetDummy` (Testing folder): shows health and last hit above itself, flashes red, revives after 3 s.
- `M_PP_DamageVignette`: one Custom (HLSL) node driving the black drowning ring and the ragged red blood edge
  from `DrownAmount`, `BloodAmount` and `BloodColor`.

## Verification so far
- Every C++ step built cleanly.
- Play-in-editor (driven over MCP):
  - The pawn spawns in the annex.
  - Entering the pool switches to swimming, and the pawn hovers when deep.
  - From just under the surface it floats up and rests with the eyes above water.
  - Held 15 m down, breath ran out, drowning damage killed the player, and the level restarted at the spawn point within about a minute.
- **Not yet tested** (needs real input): swimming controls, climbing out, the underwater sprint, the vignettes on
  screen, the wrench, the SPP-1 (firing, aiming, recoil, reloading, range by depth), ammo pickups, and the HUD.
